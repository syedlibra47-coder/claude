# Homepage — homespartner.ae

Scope: homepage (index.html). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: off-plan investors, including UK buyers via the UK–UAE desk; secondary: ready-home buyers/renters, owners listing.
Action: find a launch (developer / area / handover) and open a WhatsApp chat about a specific project.
Proof on hand: none real yet. Listings, projects, stats and reviews are illustrative and stay labelled; stats are placeholders by client decision.
Constraints: brand-pinned navy #20275C, gold gradient #E2C980→#B59E5A, Poppins, full three-diamond logo, light mode only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a developer's sales gallery: the featured off-plan launch stands as a lit architectural scale model on a white plinth, with its payment-plan wall and floorplate lightboxes beside it. It refuses the category default of a skyline photo hero over a search bar and a grid of identical listing cards.

OWN-WORLD: White gallery stone ground with cool-grey plinth surfaces; navy #20275C as engraved plaque text and model massing; brand gold as brass trim only: plaque rules, model edge light, milestone tips. Poppins Light body, Semibold plaques and headings. Components: brass-ruled plaques, floorplate lightbox cards with line-drawn plans, a milestone wall of tall bars for payment plans, plinths with a soft floor shadow. No eyebrows, no gradient text outside the logo.

STORY: An overseas investor sees one launch as a physical model, reads its handover and payment plan at a glance, filters launches by developer, area and handover, and messages the HomesPartner partner on WhatsApp about that exact project. Below: ready homes, areas, the partner and UK desk, insights, contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full logo nav on white. Left five columns: headline, one line of subcopy, the launch finder (developer, area, handover year, Find launches) with a WhatsApp link beneath. Right seven columns: the scale model on its plinth, rotating slowly, a brass plaque under it with project, developer, handover, starting price, payment plan and "Ask about this launch".

FORM: Sales gallery, position 1 on my ordered list; the user chose the pick over the dealt position 5. seed:5cc09c8946071b41. Signature: the model turns slowly (pausable), turns by drag, and swaps launch with a camera move when a launch is chosen on the plaque or from the board. Floorplans are static (taste-skill bans scroll-triggered reveals); the page's one scroll moment is the dealt word-highlight statement.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
