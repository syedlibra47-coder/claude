Buying in Dubai from the UK

The UK desk at homespartner.co.uk runs the same purchase for buyers who never fly out before handover.

  1. Video viewing

    We walk the show unit and the site with you on a video call.

  2. Reservation

    You pay the booking deposit to the developer by bank transfer.

  3. Sales agreement

    You sign the SPA electronically; we check it against the plan you were quoted.

  4. Oqood registration

    We register the sale with the Dubai Land Department.

  5. Handover inspection

    We inspect the unit at handover and send you the snag list.
